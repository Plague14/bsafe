# BSafe Business Model

## Executive Summary

BSafe is a Solana-based non-custodial multisig wallet with digital inheritance capabilities. Our business model combines a tiered membership system with transaction-based fees to create sustainable revenue while remaining accessible to all users.

---

## Value Proposition

### Problem
- **Crypto Inheritance Gap**: Over $100 billion in cryptocurrency will be lost due to death without proper succession planning
- **Complex Solutions**: Existing inheritance solutions are either too technical, expensive, or centralized
- **Solana Desert**: No dedicated inheritance solution exists for the Solana ecosystem

### Solution
BSafe provides:
- Non-custodial multisig wallet security
- Automated digital inheritance with multiple trigger mechanisms
- User-friendly interface accessible to crypto newcomers
- Affordable pricing for regular users and premium services for high-net-worth individuals

---

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Transaction Fees (Free Tier)
- **Fee**: 1% on inheritance claims
- **Target**: Regular users who want basic inheritance protection
- **Revenue per claim**: Proportional to inheritance amount

### 2. Premium Memberships
- **One-time payment**: 1 SOL (~$150 at current prices)
- **Value**: 0% claim fees, more vaults, more beneficiaries
- **Target**: Active crypto users with moderate holdings

### 3. Concierge Memberships
- **Annual subscription**: 50 SOL (~$7,500/year)
- **Value**: Full features, unlimited vaults, VIP support, custom solutions
- **Target**: High-net-worth individuals, family offices, DAOs

## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Free users**: $5-10 (organic + community)
- **Premium users**: $50-100 (targeted marketing)
- **Concierge users**: $500-1,000 (direct sales)

### Lifetime Value (LTV)
- **Free user**: $75 (1 claim average)
- **Premium user**: $150 (one-time)
- **Concierge user**: $22,500 (3-year average retention)

### LTV:CAC Ratios
- Free: 7.5:1 - 15:1
- Premium: 1.5:1 - 3:1
- Concierge: 22:1 - 45:1
